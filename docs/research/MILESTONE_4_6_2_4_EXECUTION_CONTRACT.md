**Project:** NEPTUNE Autonomous Market Intelligence & Quantitative Research Platform  
**Milestone:** 4.6.2.4  
**Title:** Point-in-Time Execution Scheduling & Backtesting Engine Integration  
**Architecture Decision Record:** ADR-026  
**Document:** Formal Execution-Time Contract Specification  
**Status:** Approved & Verified  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone 4.6.2.4 establishes an explicit, mathematically sound, and point-in-time compliant **Execution-Time Contract** for NEPTUNE's historical simulation and backtesting engine.

Prior to Milestone 4.6.2.4, baseline simulations evaluated trading candidates at candle close $T$ and instantaneously executed hypothetical fills at the exact closing price of that same candle (`curr_candle.close`). While suitable for early technical baseline benchmarking (Milestones 4.1–4.5), this legacy assumption is physically impossible in live exchange execution and introduces subtle look-ahead bias when integrating real-time news intelligence.

This document formally codifies the **Next-Eligible-Price Execution Contract** (`ExecutionPolicy.NEXT_ELIGIBLE_PRICE`), guaranteeing that no simulated order may consume future information or receive retroactive fills.

## 2. The Formal Execution-Time Contract

### 2.1 Required Order Provenance Coordinates

Every simulated order queued, scheduled, or executed within NEPTUNE must retain an immutable audit ledger comprising nine chronological coordinates:

| Coordinate | Type / Format | Definition & Invariant Guarantee |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| `signal_timestamp` | ISO-8601 UTC | Timestamp emitted by the strategy analyst candidate ($t_{\text{signal}}$). |
| `information_availability_cutoff` | ISO-8601 UTC | Upper temporal bound for all market and news data available to the decision engine ($t_{\text{avail}} \le t_{\text{cutoff}}$). |
| `candle_close_timestamp` | ISO-8601 UTC | Finalized close timestamp of candle $T$ used to compute technical indicators and candidate setups ($t_{\text{close}}$). |
| `order_creation_timestamp` | ISO-8601 UTC | Instant when the candidate setup was structured into an execution order ($t_{\text{create}} \ge t_{\text{close}}$). |
| `risk_approval_timestamp` | ISO-8601 UTC | Exact time when the Risk Manager evaluated portfolio limits and authorized the order ($t_{\text{risk}} \ge t_{\text{create}}$). |
| `risk_approval_token` | Cryptographic String | Unique cryptographic token generated by `RiskManager.evaluate_signal()`. Execution is impossible without this token. |
| `earliest_eligible_execution_timestamp` | ISO-8601 UTC | Earliest physical timestamp at which market execution is permissible ($t_{\text{earliest}} \ge t_{\text{close}}$). |
| `actual_fill_timestamp` | ISO-8601 UTC | Actual timestamp of the candle whose opening price provided the order fill ($t_{\text{fill}} \ge t_{\text{earliest}}$). |
| `actual_fill_price` | Python `Decimal` | Effective executed price after applying taker fees, bid-ask spread half-penalty, and slippage. |
| `raw_fill_price` | Python `Decimal` | Unadjusted market price used as the basis for fill calculations (`next_candle.open`). |
| `execution_policy` | String Enum | Explicit policy version under which the order was executed (`LEGACY_CLOSE_FILL` vs. `NEXT_ELIGIBLE_PRICE`). |

## 3. Core Temporal Invariants & Invalidation Rules

The execution contract enforces six non-negotiable temporal invariants:

```
[Candle T: Open -> Close] ──────────► [Candle T Close: Cutoff] ──────────► [Candle T+1 Open: Earliest Fill]
      (Price Action)                      (Indicators & Brain)                      (Next Eligible Price)
                                          (Risk Manager Check)                      (Friction & Accounting)
```

### Invariant 1: Finalized Candle Decision Cutoff
Decisions are evaluated exclusively upon the receipt of fully finalized candles. In-progress candles (`is_closed == False`) are strictly prohibited from generating trading signals (Rule A-09). The decision cutoff is formally:
$$t_{\text{cutoff}} = t_{\text{candle\_close}}(T)$$

### Invariant 2: Next-Eligible-Price Execution
Under `ExecutionPolicy.NEXT_ELIGIBLE_PRICE`, orders approved at $t_{\text{candle\_close}}(T)$ are queued into `PointInTimeExecutionScheduler` and execute strictly at Bar $T+1$ Open:
$$t_{\text{fill}} = t_{\text{candle\_open}}(T+1) \ge t_{\text{candle\_close}}(T)$$
Execution at $t_{\text{candle\_close}}(T)$ or any intrabar price of candle $T$ raises a fatal `ValueError`.

### Invariant 3: Revision-Aware Information Barrier
If an article is published at $t_1$ and revised at $t_2$, a decision made at $t_{\text{cutoff}} < t_2$ is strictly isolated from the revision. `PointInTimeExecutionSynchronizer.filter_visible_version()` returns only the latest revision available at or before $t_{\text{cutoff}}$.

### Invariant 4: Market-Data Gap Handling & Expiry
If a market-data connection interruption causes missing candles between decision close $t_{\text{close}}(T)$ and the next observed candle $t_{\text{observed}}$ such that:
$$t_{\text{observed}} - t_{\text{close}}(T) > (\text{max\_execution\_gap\_bars} + 1) \times \Delta t_{\text{timeframe}}$$
the scheduled order is automatically marked `OrderStatus.EXPIRED` and cancelled without retroactive execution.

### Invariant 5: Opening Gap Stop-Loss Protection
If market volatility causes Bar $T+1$ to open beyond the order's suggested stop loss:
- For `LONG`: $\text{Price}_{\text{open}}(T+1) \le \text{StopLoss}$
- For `SHORT`: $\text{Price}_{\text{open}}(T+1) \ge \text{StopLoss}$
the order is rejected prior to fill with reason `Market opening gap below/above stop loss`.

### Invariant 6: Real-Time Circuit Breaker Re-verification
Prior to filling a scheduled order at Bar $T+1$ Open, the scheduler re-verifies that neither the Drawdown Circuit Breaker (5.0%) nor the Daily Loss Limit (2.0%) has tripped in the interim. If tripped, the pending order is rejected immediately.

## 4. Policy Versioning & Backward Compatibility

To preserve scientific reproducibility and prevent the invalidation of existing technical benchmarks (Milestones 4.1 through 4.5.1), execution policies are formally versioned in `BacktestConfig`:

1. **`ExecutionPolicy.LEGACY_CLOSE_FILL`:**
   - Retains the exact legacy behavior where trades fill at `curr_candle.close`.
   - Default setting for `BacktestConfig()` to ensure 100% regression compatibility across all historical unit tests.
   - **Restriction:** If a simulation configures news inputs while set to `LEGACY_CLOSE_FILL`, the engine raises a fatal `ValueError` requiring explicit transition to `NEXT_ELIGIBLE_PRICE`.

2. **`ExecutionPolicy.NEXT_ELIGIBLE_PRICE`:**
   - Mandatory policy for all news-gated backtests, event studies, and live paper execution.
   - Enforces the full point-in-time execution contract and schedules fills at the next available candle open.
