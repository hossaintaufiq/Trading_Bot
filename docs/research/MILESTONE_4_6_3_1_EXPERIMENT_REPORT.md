## 1. Executive Summary & Authoritative Verdict

Milestone 4.6.3.1 executes the first controlled, reproducible experiment of the NEPTUNE quantitative research program using the frozen research protocol.

The central research question investigated is:
> **Does point-in-time news vetoing improve net trading outcomes beyond the frozen deterministic technical baseline, after accounting for trading frequency, market exposure, fees, spread, slippage, and risk constraints?**

### Authoritative Verdict: **FAIL (No Statistically Significant Alpha or Positive Net Expectancy)**

1. **Failure to Achieve Positive Net Trading Expectancy:**
   - Neither the technical baseline nor the news-veto treatment strategy achieved positive net expectancy after transaction costs (0.10% taker fee, 0.05% adverse slippage, 1.0 bps bid-ask spread = 0.32% round trip).
   - Group A (Technical Baseline): Net PnL of **-$214.72 USDT**, Profit Factor **0.49**, Net Expectancy $E_{\text{trade}} = -\$3.1576$ USDT, $E_{\text{cand}} = -\$0.0584$ USDT.
   - Group B (News-Veto Treatment): Net PnL of **-$114.42 USDT**, Profit Factor **0.64**, Net Expectancy $E_{\text{trade}} = -\$2.4345$ USDT, $E_{\text{cand}} = -\$0.0311$ USDT.
2. **Failure of Confirmatory Hypothesis Testing (Bootstrap $p > 0.05$):**
   - **Hypothesis 1 (Group B vs Group A):** Although nominal $E_{\text{trade}}$ improved by $+0.7232$ USDT (nominally exceeding the $+\$0.50$ hurdle), the 384-bar moving block bootstrap ($B=2000$) reveals an empirical $p$-value of **$0.5950$**. With Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction ($q=0.05$), the null hypothesis $H_0$ **cannot be rejected**. The nominal improvement is statistically indistinguishable from random sampling variance.
   - **Hypothesis 2 (Group B vs Group C - Frequency Control):** Mean candidate expectancy difference $\Delta E_{\text{cand}} = +0.0195$ USDT with bootstrap $p$-value **$0.5950$** (FDR insignificant).
   - **Hypothesis 3 (Group B vs Group D - Exposure Control):** Mean candidate expectancy difference $\Delta E_{\text{cand}} = +0.0137$ USDT with bootstrap $p$-value **$0.5900$** (FDR insignificant).
3. **The Exposure & Activity Confounding Discovery:**
   - News vetoing rejected 183 candidate signals (4.98% of the candidate stream), which reduced executed trades from 68 to 47 (a 30.88% reduction in trading activity).
   - Time in market dropped from 824 bars (2.35%) in Group A to 659 bars (1.88%) in Group B. Dollar-hours capital utilization decreased from $133,753.30 to $101,791.55.
   - The observed reduction in dollar loss (from -$214.72 to -$114.42) was predominantly driven by **reduced exposure to a negative-drift technical engine**, identical in nature to the non-news exposure controls (Group C lost -$186.21; Group D lost -$164.80).

## 4. Confirmatory Hypothesis Testing & Bootstrap Analysis

A Moving Block Bootstrap preserving 4-day serial autocorrelation ($L = 384$ bars on 15m, $B = 2,000$ iterations, seed 42) was conducted on matched candidate pairs across all 3,678 candidates:

$$\Delta_i = \text{PnL}_B(c_i) - \text{PnL}_{\text{control}}(c_i)$$

```
                               Hypothesis Test Results Matrix
 ┌──────────────────────┬─────────────┬─────────────┬──────────────┬──────────┬───────────┐
 │ Hypothesis           │ Metric      │ Observed    │ 95% Boot CI  │ p-value  │ FDR Sig   │
 ├──────────────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────┼──────────────┼──────────┼───────────┤
 │ H1: Group B vs A     │ Δ E_cand    │ +$0.0273    │ [0.00, 0.05] │ 0.5950   │ FAIL      │
 │ H2: Group B vs C     │ Δ E_cand    │ +$0.0195    │ [0.00, 0.04] │ 0.5950   │ FAIL      │
 │ H3: Group B vs D     │ Δ E_cand    │ +$0.0137    │ [0.00, 0.03] │ 0.5900   │ FAIL      │
 └──────────────────────┴─────────────┴─────────────┴──────────────┴──────────┴───────────┘
```

### Benjamini-Hochberg False Discovery Rate ($q = 0.05$) Correction

| Rank | Hypothesis | Raw $p$-value | Critical Value $(k/m \cdot q)$ | Significant under FDR? |
|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | **H3 (Group B vs D)** | 0.5900 | $1/3 \times 0.05 = 0.0167$ | **NO** |
| 2 | **H1 (Group B vs A)** | 0.5950 | $2/3 \times 0.05 = 0.0333$ | **NO** |
| 3 | **H2 (Group B vs C)** | 0.5950 | $3/3 \times 0.05 = 0.0500$ | **NO** |

**Conclusion on Hypotheses:** Every confirmatory hypothesis fails to achieve statistical significance ($p \approx 0.59 \gg 0.05$).

### Economic Hurdle Evaluation

- Pre-registered economic hurdle: $\Delta \mathbb{E}_{\text{trade}} \ge +0.50$ USDT.
- Observed $\Delta \mathbb{E}_{\text{trade}} = -2.4345 - (-3.1576) = \mathbf{+0.7232}$ USDT.
- **Scientific Audit Finding:** While the point estimate numerically satisfies the nominal $+0.50$ USDT threshold, the underlying distribution has a bootstrap $p$-value of $0.5950$ and includes $0.00$. Therefore, the nominal outperformance cannot be affirmed as economically genuine; it is indistinguishable from random trade selection in a small sample ($N_B = 47, N_A = 68$).

## 6. Retrospective Ex-Post Diagnostic Benchmarking

| Method | Diagnostic Technique | Selected Trades | Net Realized PnL | $E_{\text{trade}}$ | $E_{\text{cand}}$ |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Method C1** | Systematic Decimation | 47 | -$0.44 USDT | -$0.0094 | -$0.0001 |
| **Method C2** | Technical Confidence Priority | 47 | **+$4.19 USDT** | **+$0.0891** | **+$0.0011** |
| **Method C3** | Cryptographic Seed Selection | 47 | -$0.51 USDT | -$0.0109 | -$0.0001 |
| **Method D-ExPost** | Cumulative Exposure Budget | 44 | $0.00 USDT | $0.0000 | $0.0000 |

### Critical Diagnostic Insight
- Retrospective Method C2 (selecting candidates based purely on technical confidence scores) achieved a net positive PnL (+4.19 USDT, $E_{\text{trade}} = +\$0.0891$), whereas News Vetoing remained deeply negative (-$114.42 USDT, $E_{\text{trade}} = -\$2.4345$).
- This confirms that **news vetoing in 2024 did not systematically isolate toxic trades more effectively than technical confidence sorting**.
