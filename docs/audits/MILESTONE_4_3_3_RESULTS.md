## 1. Executive Verdict

### **Verdict**: **PASS WITH LIMITATIONS**

The quantitative benchmark reconciliation, accounting audit, and candidate-level replay engine have passed rigorous mathematical and empirical verification. The implementation is clean, deterministic, reproducible, free of look-ahead bias, and adheres strictly to the modular monolith architecture and paper-trading principles.

However, the pass is granted **WITH EXPLICIT QUANTITATIVE LIMITATIONS** regarding the statistical interpretation of the results:

1. **H1 Baseline Duplication is Legitimate**: Baseline and H1 produce identical metrics because ADX $\ge$ 25 is an upstream prerequisite in `MarketScannerAgent` for declaring `TRENDING_BULL` or `TRENDING_BEAR`. H1 is a redundant filter.
2. **Accounting is Exact Down to the Cent**: The underlying Decimal accounting in `PaperExecutionSimulator` is bit-for-bit exact (`gross - fees - slippage - spread - net == 0.00`). Discrepancies between the sum of displayed components and net PnL are bounded to $\pm \$0.01$ per trade due to independent half-up cent quantizing (`ROUND_HALF_UP`). The $27.14 vs $27.00 spread figure was a display formatting omission in `run_milestone_4_3_2_parallel.py` (`round(tot_spread)` without decimal places), not an accounting error.
3. **Architectural Coupling of Candidate Generation**: In the current engine, candidate evaluations are portfolio-gated (`max_concurrent_positions = 2`). Suppressing losing trades unblocks the portfolio, increasing candidate evaluations from 770 (Baseline) to 991 (H7).
4. **H7 Fails Bonferroni Multiple-Testing Significance**: When correcting for the family of 18 explored pipelines ($K = 18$), the significance threshold drops to $\alpha_{\text{Bonferroni}} \approx 0.00278$. H7's observed p-values do not clear this hurdle.
5. **4h Outlier Sensitivity**: The 4h Test set net profit of +$6.04 is entirely driven by two correlated short trades entered during a single macro crash on May 28–29, 2026. Without these two trades, the 4h test set loses -$35.59 (with BNBUSDT losing -$20.82 alone).

## 2. Answers to the Six Mandatory Audit Questions

### Question 1: Why did Baseline and H1 produce identical results in Milestone 4.3.2?
**Answer**:  
In `backend/app/agents/market_scanner.py` (lines 137–147), the market scanner requires `curr_adx >= 25.0` to classify a market regime as `TRENDING_BULL` or `TRENDING_BEAR`. In `backend/app/agents/strategy_analyst.py` (lines 143–169), candidate signals are only emitted if the regime is `TRENDING_BULL` (for LONG) or `TRENDING_BEAR` (for SHORT).  
Consequently, every candidate signal generated by the baseline strategy already possessed an ADX of at least 25.01 (maximum observed: 71.68). The `H1_ADX_25` entry filter evaluated all 770 candidate signals and rejected exactly 0. H1 was a **redundant filter** testing an already-enforced prerequisite. When tested with a stricter threshold (`ADX >= 30.0` in `H1b_ADX_30`), 317 candidates were rejected and trades dropped to 194, proving the filtering mechanism is fully functional.

### Question 2: How many candidates does NEPTUNE actually generate when evaluated unconditionally across all bars, versus how many are evaluated when portfolio-gated, accepted by filters, authorized by risk, and executed?
**Answer**:  
Because NEPTUNE's engine checks portfolio slot occupancy (`len(open_positions) >= 2`) before invoking `StrategyAnalystAgent`, the number of evaluated bars depends on how much time the portfolio spends holding open trades.
- **Unconditional Raw Market Setup Opportunities**: Evaluated independently across all 38,730 evaluable candles without portfolio constraints, the market generated **1,137 raw strategy setups** across the 9 series.
- **Portfolio-Gated Candidate Signals**:
  - Baseline: Evaluated 770 candidates (28,361 bars evaluated, 10,369 bars suppressed by active positions).
  - H7 Confluence: Evaluated 991 candidates (30,116 bars evaluated, 8,614 bars suppressed by active positions).
- **Filter-Accepted Signals**:
  - Baseline: 770 accepted (0 rejected).
  - H7 Confluence: 182 accepted (809 rejected).
- **Risk-Approved Signals**:
  - Baseline: 277 approved (493 rejected due to slot/margin limits).
  - H7 Confluence: 126 approved (56 rejected due to slot/margin limits).
- **Executed Trades**:
  - Baseline: 277 executed.
  - H7 Confluence: 126 executed.

### Question 3: Is the accounting across all models exact down to the cent, and what caused the $27.14 vs $27.00 spread discrepancy?
**Answer**:  
Yes. Internally, `PaperExecutionSimulator` performs all arithmetic using Python `Decimal` with full precision, strictly satisfying $\text{Net PnL} = \text{Gross PnL} - \text{Fees} - \text{Slippage} - \text{Spread}$.  
When persisting trade ledgers and computing summary reports, each component is independently quantized to cents via `Decimal("0.01")` using `ROUND_HALF_UP`. This independent rounding convention causes an expected $\pm \$0.01$ sub-cent discrepancy per trade, accumulating to -$0.08 over 277 trades in Baseline.  
The $27.14 vs $27.00 spread discrepancy was caused by line 240 of `scratch/run_milestone_4_3_2_parallel.py`: `"spread": round(tot_spread),` which omitted `, 2`, thereby rounding the exact Decimal spread ($27.142857...) to the integer $27. The parquet event ledger always stored the true unrounded cost.

### Question 4: Does H7 maintain statistical significance after Bonferroni correction for all 18 evaluated pipelines?
**Answer**:  
**No.** Milestones 4.3 and 4.3.2 evaluated 9 distinct filter hypotheses (H0 Baseline, H1, H1b, H2, H3, H4a, H4b, H5, H6, H7) across 2 exit policies (BALANCED, FIXED_BRACKET), representing $K = 18$ distinct trading pipelines.  
To preserve a family-wise error rate of $\alpha = 0.05$, the Bonferroni significance threshold is $\alpha_{\text{Bonferroni}} = 0.05 / 18 \approx 0.00278$. The paired and independent p-values for H7 exceed this threshold ($p = 0.5734$ on 90 matched trades). Furthermore, circular block bootstrap resampling ($B = 5$, 2,000 replications, Seed 42) yields a 95% confidence interval for H7 net expectancy of [-$0.84, +$1.16] with an empirical probability of net profitability of only 56.8%. H7 does not maintain statistical significance after multiple-testing correction.

### Question 5: What does the trade-level breakdown of the 4h Test set reveal about the source of its positive PnL?
**Answer**:  
The 4h Test set (May 1, 2026 to July 1, 2026) executed 24 trades with a net profit of +$6.04.  
A granular symbol-level inspection reveals:
- **BTCUSDT**: 4 trades, Net PnL = **+$18.82** (Win Rate: 50.0%)
- **ETHUSDT**: 4 trades, Net PnL = **+$8.04** (Win Rate: 50.0%)
- **BNBUSDT**: 16 trades, Net PnL = **-$20.82** (Win Rate: 31.2%)

**Outlier Sensitivity**:  
- The single highest-performing trade was a BTCUSDT SHORT (`trd_d6f0ad8e3bae`) entered on May 28, 2026, which captured +$26.60. Excluding this one trade drops 4h Test PnL from +$6.04 to **-$20.56**.
- The second highest trade was an ETHUSDT SHORT (`trd_93ec327e5e41`) entered on May 29, 2026, which captured +$15.03. Both trades were closed on the exact same candle (`2026-06-04T04:00:00Z`).
- Excluding both trades drops 4h Test PnL to **-$35.59**.
The apparent edge in the 4h Test set was not a pervasive, symbol-agnostic trend edge, but rather two correlated short positions entered during a single market-wide crash event.

### Question 6: What is the final quantitative verdict on H7 and entry filtering in NEPTUNE?
**Answer**:  
H7 Confluence is **mechanically and architecturally sound as a volatility/trend gating filter** that effectively prunes low-volatility, range-bound false breakouts (cutting trade volume from 277 to 126 and saving $227 in losses). However, **H7 cannot be declared a statistically validated, profitable trading strategy**. Its modest positive net PnL (+$14.02 over 6 months) lacks statistical significance after multiple-testing adjustments, is highly sensitive to single-event outlier clusters, and does not overcome the fundamental hurdle that trend breakout strategies suffer negative expectancy during prolonged choppy market regimes under realistic taker fees and slippage.
